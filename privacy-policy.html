Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

4A Store ("we", "us", "our") operates the 4A Store website (4astore.com) and the 4A Store Android app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our app or website, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: your name and mobile number when you register or place an order.
  • Delivery details: the delivery address and PIN code you provide for your order.
  • Order details: the items you order, amounts, and payment method (UPI).
  • Payment proof: a payment screenshot you upload to confirm a UPI payment.
  • Device/usage data: basic technical information (like app version) needed to run the service.

We do not collect your bank passwords, UPI PIN, card numbers, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your grocery orders.
  • To contact you about your order (confirmation, delivery, support).
  • To improve our products, service, and delivery.
  • To send offers or announcements (only within the app/website).

3. Payments

Payments are made directly via UPI to our store account. We only receive your payment confirmation/screenshot. We do not store card or UPI credentials.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share order/delivery details only with the delivery person fulfilling your order, and where required by law.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your order and account information is stored on our server and used only to run the store. We take reasonable steps to protect it. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

6. Your Choices

  • You can ask us to update or delete your account information.
  • You can stop using the app at any time.
  • For any data request, contact us using the details below.

7. Children

Our service is intended for users aged 18 and above. Some products are age-restricted and require the buyer to confirm they are 18+.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
