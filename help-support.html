Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse products, add them to your cart, go to checkout, enter your delivery address, pay via UPI (scan the QR or copy the UPI ID), upload the payment screenshot, and confirm your order.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We currently deliver within PIN code 824301 (Chandargarh, Nabinagar area). More locations coming soon.

How do I pay?

Payment is via UPI. Scan the QR code shown at payment or copy our UPI ID, pay from any UPI app, then upload the payment screenshot to confirm.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, contact us as soon as possible before the order is dispatched. If you already paid for something we can't deliver, we'll refund it via UPI.

What if I receive a damaged or wrong item?

Contact us the same day with a photo. We'll replace it or refund the amount.

How do I see my past orders?

Open "Orders" in the app to view your order history and download invoices.
